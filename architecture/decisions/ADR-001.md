# One modular product with an independent core

## Decision and rationale
Keep Core independent of features. Atlas, Guard and Observatory share typed snapshots. CLI composes them. Briefcase remains external. Separate services would add coordination before there is an operational need.

## Alternatives
Duplicated authority or implicit approval would obscure review and evidence boundaries.
