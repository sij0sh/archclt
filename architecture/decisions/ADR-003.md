# Preserve evidence and intent as separate authorities

## Decision and rationale
Observed relationships retain source pins, provenance and coverage limits. They can challenge intent but never overwrite approved decisions. Native Briefcase compatibility must be validated against actual schemas. Successful tests do not create runtime observations.

## Alternatives
Duplicated authority or implicit approval would obscure review and evidence boundaries.
